Welcome to the course!

This blockchain basics course isn't only for developers; it's also for anyone who wants to understand how blockchain works.

Before you start, here's a quick overview of how the course works.

### What you'll do here

Each course consists of lessons of four types:

- **Lectures**: you're on one right now.
- **Tests**: a list of questions with answer options; you select the correct ones.
- **Coding tasks**: not part of this course. In dev-oriented courses, you write code in a browser editor and submit it; it's reviewed automatically with AI and pre-defined tests.
- **Projects**: larger versions of coding tasks. Instead of writing code in the browser, you build something in your own GitHub repo and submit a link. Projects are reviewed the same way: automatically, with AI and pre-defined tests.

Coding tasks and projects are not about syntax knowledge; they're built to develop the way a Web3 engineer actually thinks. We've used this format for years in our private blockchain academy, previously open only to trainees and employees. Now it's open to everyone.

### How your progress is tracked

- At the bottom of each lesson, the **Go to next lesson** button also marks the lesson as complete.
- You get a certificate once you've completed every lesson in the course.
- Your leaderboard rank is based on the total number of lessons you've completed.

### Navigation

- The **Continue** button on the home page takes you back to the last lesson you were on.
- Use the course outline on the left to jump between lessons.

### A few tips

- Try to spot the patterns in each lesson instead of memorizing answers. Look for the underlying way of thinking behind each case.
- Take your time. This course isn't about learning syntax quickly. It's meant to build the mindset behind good engineering, not hand you a few coding tricks.

Let's get started!
