Welcome to the Blockchain Development Course by RedDuck Academy! 

This course is applicable to both developers and blockchain enthusiasts. Before you start, here is an overview of how the course works.

### What you will do

Each course consists of lessons of four types:

- **Lectures**: you're on one right now.
- **Tests**: lists of questions with answering options for you to select the correct one.
- **Coding tasks**: Not applicable for the blockchain basics course. For dev-oriented courses, you will be requested to write code in a browser editor and submit it. The code is reviewed automatically with the use of AI and pre-defined tests.
- **Projects**: Same as coding tasks, but larger scale. The projects are larger builds in your own GitHub repo that you submit as a link, instead of writing the code directly in the browser. Same as with coding tasks, the projects also get reviewed automatically with the use of AI and pre-defined tests.

The programming tasks/projects build up intrinsic understanding of a successful Web3 engineer's mindset, which is why we've had them in our private blockchain academy for years, open to trainees and employees only. Now they're open to everyone.

### How your progress is tracked

- You click the **Go to the next lesson** button at the bottom of each article to mark it as completed.
- You only get the certificate after you complete all lessons within each course.
- Your rank on the leaderboard reflects how many lessons you've completed.

### Navigation

- The **Continue** button on the home page directs you to the last lesson where you left off.
- Use the course outline on the left to move between lessons of a course.

### A few tips

- Try inferring patterns from the lessons. Don't just memorize the answers, rather try to extract the ultimate thinking pattern applicable to a particular case.
- Take your time. This isn't a course to learn syntax quickly or get a few tips on coding; it is built to harden your mindset. 

Let's get to it!
