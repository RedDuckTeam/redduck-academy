title: Break the system as an adversary
type: review_task
order: 2
---

You are given a service that behaves correctly under normal use and shows no obvious flaw. Take the position of a rational adversary and find a way to extract value you are not owed, using only the public interface. Prove it with a working test. Then explain the flaw in terms of the system's design, and say whether your fix patches the attack or removes the condition that made it possible.
