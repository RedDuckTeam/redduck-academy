title: Find and fix a common-sense flaw
type: review_task
order: 5
---

You are handed a small service with one hidden flaw. Somewhere it trusts a value that the caller fully controls. Run the common-sense check on it, find the design a self-interested caller would walk straight through, and fix it. You are graded on the explanation of the adversarial move as much as on the fix itself.
