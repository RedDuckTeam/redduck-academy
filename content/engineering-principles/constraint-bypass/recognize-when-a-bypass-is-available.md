title: Recognize when a bypass is available
type: review_task
order: 3
---

You are given three design problems. Each one has an obvious solution that works inside a hard constraint. For each problem, either find a way to make the constraint irrelevant, or show that the constraint is a real property of the outcome and cannot be bypassed. At least one of the three has no bypass, and recognizing that is part of the task.
