The mindset behind good work, drawn from the principles RedDuck runs on. It teaches ways of thinking about problems, communication, and responsibility that turn most blockers back into ordinary tasks you can handle yourself. You'll learn to orient toward the solution, to own what you commit to, and to communicate so clearly that nobody has to pull the words out of you. Every idea here holds up at work and in the rest of your life.
